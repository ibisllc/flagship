Flagship 0.4 Beta opens the things that were waiting: you can now call the people who visit your page, give
an app a direct address that skips our relay, and pay for a bigger plan without telling us who you are.

Flagship stays free. The Free plan keeps every feature it had; Pro and Pro Max add room and a few extras.

## What you can do now

### Calls from your page

- **People can call you from voi.ci/<your name>.** Audio first, and video when both of you turn it on. Your
  phone rings like a phone call, even when it is locked, and you can answer from your iPad too.
- **Call back from Social.** A missed call shows in the conversation and in Activity; call back from there.
- **You choose who can call:** people you have talked to, anyone signed in with Flagship, or anyone at all
  (with spam limits), or turn calls off. Call hours keep your phone quiet at night; they stay on your own devices.
- **Calls are end-to-end encrypted** and go straight to your phone. Flagship sees that a call happened and how
  long it lasted, not what was said.
- **Names on a locked phone, if you want them.** Turn on "Show names while locked" and a ringing call can show the
  nickname you gave the caller, or the page calling you back, while Flagship is locked. These names stay on that
  phone. The privacy policy (§2.12, Calls on voi.ci) now says so, and describes the short code a call-back's ring
  carries and the key that lets your locked phone recognise the page.

### Social, the new home of your page

- **Messages is now Social.** Your page is the tab: you see it as visitors do, edit each part where it is, and
  publish when you're ready. Messages and calls sit beside it.
- **Activity shows calls and messages** with your servers' events, and deleting a conversation removes it from
  every device of your account.

### Plans, paid privately

- **Pro and Pro Max.** Pro adds 250 GB of public traffic and one custom domain, Pro Max 500 GB and as many
  domains as you like. Free stays at 25 GB, slowed (never cut) past it.
- **Paid with $1 vouchers.** Buy a pack in the app or on the web; your phone keeps the vouchers and spends them on
  your plan. Our server signs them blind, so it never learns which account a purchase paid for.
- **Use your own domain** on Pro, and **change your account name** (a paid change).
- **Send your server's traffic through your own proxy** (Pro). If our private relay is ever down, nothing
  stops: payments wait and go direct before your plan would lapse. If your proxy fails, the app tells you at
  once and the fix always gets through.

### Business, for teams

- **Organizations with several admins.** Seats, admins scoped to some servers or apps, approvals that need
  several admins, freezing a seat, the organization's own rules, re-keying when someone leaves, an audit export
  you can check offline, and SCIM and LDAP directory connectors.
- **$6 a seat a month, at least 3 seats,** with 100 GB of public traffic per seat shared by the whole
  organization. A new organization gets 10 seats for 30 days; paid time starts when the trial ends.
- **The organization's own wallet,** held by its admins and encrypted under a key only they share. It pays a
  little at a time, like a personal plan. If payment stops, nothing is lost: every seat, app and record stays,
  and only adding people, servers or connectors waits.
- **An invoice for the company, not its people.** A company can buy a batch of 100 vouchers or more on the web,
  with an invoice in its name and tax ID that names no organization, account or seat. The batch goes into the
  organization's wallet, or into voucher files staff spend on their own Pro.

### Direct access: skip the relay

- **Give an app a direct address** like `game--you.fshp.app` when your server can be reached from the internet
  (IPv6, or IPv4 with a forwarded port). Visitors connect straight to your server; encryption still ends on it,
  and the traffic doesn't count against your plan.
- **Only what you sign is published.** Turn a name off and its record goes. We never point a direct name at our
  relay, and never move it while your server is down.

### More control

- **Restore into a server you already have.** Pick app by app what to install, keep or replace from a backup,
  with an undo for seven days.
- **The webapp grows up.** On a computer or tablet it has a sidebar and side-by-side lists. It can keep your
  admin key wrapped by a passkey.
- **Share with more people on bigger plans:** co-managers per account are 5 on Free, 10 on Pro, 25 on Pro Max.
- **Notifications without Google** on Android, through UnifiedPush (for example ntfy).
- **A Firefox extension** checks that a server's certificate is the one your phone pinned, and helps you sign
  in to your members-only apps.
- **Quantum-safe signatures reach further:** the admin key and device grants now carry a post-quantum half.

### Fixes worth knowing

- Fixes from our Windows Studio testing, and a new server that restarts during setup keeps its certificate
  order instead of starting it again.
- Studios out of date say so and stop, instead of showing new pairing codes.
- Wear OS: the tile and the complication are translated, and the tile shows what to do when the phone has no
  servers yet.

## Coming from 0.3

Update the app, then update each server from its page when it offers 0.4. The new features need both.

## Known limits

- Calls need the 0.4 app on the phone you call; a page whose owner has an older app shows no Call button.
- Direct names share Let's Encrypt's weekly budget for `fshp.app` until our rate-limit increase is granted, so a
  new name can wait a few days for its certificate.
- Vouchers bought in one store stay in that phone's wallet until it syncs them to your other devices.
