Flagship 0.5 Beta. This file grows as 0.5 is built.

## What you can do now

### Business, for teams

- **Invitations are tied to their link.** Only someone who holds the invitation link can open it or accept it;
  knowing the organization is not enough. You still compare the six-digit code before you let anyone in.
- **Withdraw an invitation.** An admin can withdraw an invitation nobody has been let in on yet. The link stops
  working, and anyone who accepted it is not let in. If the codes don't match, the app says that someone other than
  the person you invited accepted, and offers to withdraw it.

## Coming from 0.4

Update the app, then update each server from its page when it offers 0.5.

- **People you invited to your apps: invite them again.** The private identity a visitor shows an app is now tied to
  the account that runs the app as well, so two different owners running the same app can't tell they share a
  visitor. Members who joined before 0.5 are asked for a new invitation link once their app is updated; you and your
  own devices are not affected.
- **Organization admins: send pending invitations again.** Invitations made before 0.5 that nobody was let in on
  stop working when 0.5 reaches Flagship's servers. Seats already admitted are not affected.
