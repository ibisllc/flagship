Money & subscriptions

Hardware is one-time. Subscriptions are optional. Bring-your-own-hardware is free forever.

What does Flagship cost?

Bring-your-own-hardware: $0. We're not selling boxes or subscriptions yet; when we are, we'll publish the details and they'll be opt-in for power-user surfaces only. Most people pay nothing, forever.

Why charge anything if it's "your hardware"?

Three real costs: shipping the pre-built boxes (margin, not profit-from-pricing), running the tunnel hub on Fly (a few cents per server per month at our scale), and running the registrar of flagship.services wildcard DNS. The free tier covers the steady-state for an individual on their own hardware. Subscriptions cover the things we'd otherwise have to subsidize.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancellation is one tap from the phone. We prorate the unused portion of the current period. Your subdomain stays yours; the routing entry continues to work; only the billed add-ons (push at scale, peer-backup pool, etc.) shut off. We don't ransom your DNS.

What's your refund policy on hardware?

30 days, full refund, you ship it back. We pay return shipping inside the US; outside the US it's on you. If the box arrived dead or DOA we pay both ways. We don't restock-fee. Full terms at /terms.

Do you take crypto?

Not yet. Stripe takes cards (USD, EUR, GBP). We're exploring monero / lightning for the privacy-conscious, but it's not on the immediate roadmap. If this matters to you, mail support@flagshipserver.com and tell us. That's how features get prioritized.

Will the price change?

Hardware: probably, with component costs. Existing orders are locked in. Subscriptions: we'll grandfather every existing subscriber on a price change for at least 12 months and we'll tell you at signup if a plan is "introductory." We don't do "your renewal is now triple."

How do you make money long-term?

Hardware margin, subscriptions on the optional add-ons. We don't sell ads, telemetry, model-training rights, or your data: partly because it's the right thing, mostly because we structurally can't.
