Connect this desktop

Open the Flagship app on your phone and scan this QR code. Your phone holds the keys; this desktop never gets them. Authorized operations are signed by your phone.

starting session…

How does this work?

Your browser generated a one-time session id and posted it to flagshipserver.com. The QR code embeds flagship://desktop/<sessionId>/<ephemeralPublicKey>. When you scan with the phone app and tap "Approve", your phone signs a pairing claim with your IRK (the same key that gates server boot, image rebuilds, and account-level operations).

flagshipserver.com only stores opaque session metadata. After pairing, the desktop and phone share a key that flagshipserver.com has never seen — sensitive operations from this desktop become signed RPCs to your phone.

Don't have the phone app yet?

Flagship is currently in development. The mobile clients are scaffolded under apps/mobile/ios/ and apps/mobile/android/ in the repo. For development you can simulate a phone at /dev/phone.html.
