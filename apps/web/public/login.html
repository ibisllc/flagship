Open the Flagship app on your phone (or the webapp) and scan the QR. Your primary device holds the keys; this desktop never gets them.

How does this work?

Your browser generated a one-time session id and posted it to flagshipserver.com. The QR embeds flagship://desktop/<sessionId>/<ephemeralPublicKey>. When you scan and approve on your primary device, that device signs a pairing claim with the IRK (the same key that gates boot, image rebuilds, and account-level operations).

flagshipserver.com only stores opaque session metadata. After pairing, the desktop and primary share a key flagshipserver.com has never seen — sensitive ops become signed RPCs to your primary device.

No primary device yet?

The native iOS / Android apps are in development. You can use the installable webapp as a primary device today, or simulate a phone at /dev/phone.html for development.
