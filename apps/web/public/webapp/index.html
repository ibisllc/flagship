Reattach progress

        

          

            After a recovery rebinds your identity, the daemon walks every installed app and rewrites membership rows from the OLD IRK to the NEW one. The OLD pubkey is then wiped from app data; a 7-day encrypted journal lets you undo the rewrite if needed.
          

        

        

        

          
          
        

      


      
      

        
Recovery

        

          

            Cloud recovery (passkey): wrap your master key with a passkey's PRF output and upload the ciphertext to flagshipserver.com. The unwrap key never leaves your browser; flagshipserver.com only ever sees opaque ciphertext.
