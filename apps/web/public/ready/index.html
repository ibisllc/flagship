Build your boot USB with the Flagship Studio

Three quick steps for your server. The Builder fetches the right base OS for you and bakes your recipe in — you don't pick or download an OS image, and there's no balenaEtcher, Rufus, or dd.

1 Grab your recipe

A tiny signed file — your phone authorized it, and only your phone can.

Copy if the Builder is on this computer — nothing touches the disk. Download if you'll carry it to another machine.

2 Download the Flagship Studio

The desktop app that writes your boot USB. You only install it once — reuse it for every server, just feed it a fresh recipe each time.

3 Assemble & boot

Open the Builder, paste this recipe (or open the downloaded file), pick your USB drive, and hit Assemble. Boot the target machine from the USB — the rest is automatic, ending at a real green padlock on your domain.
