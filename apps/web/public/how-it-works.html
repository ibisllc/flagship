Install

How a Flagship server is born.

Five stages. The phone signs every step. We never see your data — even when you're literally registering for the first time.

STAGE 01
Pair your phone

The phone is the keychain.

You install the Flagship app. It generates a master key inside your phone's secure enclave (Touch ID / Face ID / Android StrongBox). That key never leaves your phone. From it we derive three more: one for identity (your username), one for backup-authorization (LUKS unlock), one for working keys (per-server secrets).

You pick a username. The phone signs a registration request with the identity key. flagshipserver.com records the username + the public half. That's it — no email, no password, no SMS.

phone → flagshipserver.com# RegisterUser{username, irkPub}, IRK-signed .com → D1: usernames# PRIMARY KEY username
STAGE 02
Shows a QR scan

One-time blob the box trusts.

Your phone shows a QR scan: a small JSON containing the serverDomain you picked, your phone's delegated pubkey (so the box can verify orders later), and a 24-hour expiry. The phone signs it with your master identity key.

You can paste the QR scan at the QR on the homepage in your browser to download a personalized installer ISO. Or, if you ordered a pre-built box from us, the box fetches it automatically when you plug it in.

phone → auth-code blob (signed)# 24h, single-use browser → // → ISO with trailer# personalized
STAGE 03
Boot & install

The box installs itself in five minutes.

The ISO boots Alpine Linux on your hardware. A first-boot script validates the trailer signature locally, then fetches the public installer from our GitHub repo at the exact ref the trailer pinned. Anyone can audit what the install does — it's a few hundred lines of shell.

The installer formats your disk with LUKS, generates a per-box identity, requests a real Let's Encrypt cert, registers the server with flagshipserver.com, and boots into steady state. Your phone, watching the install, lights up: "Your Flagship is online."

box → cryptsetup luksFormat# 64-byte random key box → seal-for-bak (your phone's pubkey)# X25519 sealed-box box → flagshipserver.com /api/server/register box → Let's Encrypt (DNS-01 via .com)# wildcard cert box → tunnel hub (.services WS)# inbound traffic ingress
STAGE 04
Every-boot unlock

No phone, no decryption.

When the box reboots, the LUKS-encrypted root won't open. The boot stage polls flagshipserver.com for an unsealed unlock key. Your phone, woken by a push, asks for biometrics, decrypts the sealed blob locally, and deposits the unsealed key with a 10-minute TTL. The box consumes it; it's deleted; the disk opens.

A stolen box without your phone is a brick. Even we can't help — we never had the unsealed key.

box ⟳ POST /api/server/<fqdn>/unlock-key/consume# every 1s .com → 404 (no deposit yet) phone → face-ID prompt → unseal locally → POST DepositUnlockKey# 10-min TTL box ← 200 + unsealed key# row deleted on read box → cryptsetup luksOpen → mount → boot
STAGE 05
Live. Vibe-code. Share.

The fun part.

From the phone you describe an app in plain language. An LLM writes it (your provider key, or our daily-promo). The daemon builds the container, provisions Postgres / object store / KV namespaces scoped to the app, deploys it, registers a subdomain. Within a minute it's live at plants.harry.flagship.services.

Sharing is one tap: a magic-link invite or a username search. Members hit the same URL and your box authenticates them via headers it injects after a membership check. Apps never see cookies; they never have to handle auth. Listing on the marketplace is two more taps.

Updates pull every 6 hours from the canonical creator's pod, signed by their box. If the creator force-pushes (lineage break), every subscriber's phone gets an alert before anything bad happens.

SAFETY NET
If you lose the phone

Recovery is honest.

Your master key is wrapped with a key that lives in iCloud Keychain (iOS) or Google Block Store (Android). On a fresh phone you can re-derive it with biometrics on the new device + passcode. If you didn't enroll cloud recovery, or you lose access to that account, the data is gone. We tell you this during setup, in plain words.

For higher-stakes households, v2 will offer social recovery: split your master key across N friends with a K-of-N threshold. We're not pretending it's done yet.
