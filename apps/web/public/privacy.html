Privacy policy.

Flagship is built so we can't read your data. This page is the formal version of that promise — what we collect, what we don't, why, and how to ask for it back.

Last updated: 2026-05-11 · Effective: 2026-05-11

The short version

Read the rest if you need the legal text. The substance is here.

What we collect

  • Your username
  • Your server's public key
  • A sealed LUKS blob (we can't open it)
  • Push tokens, if you opt into push
  • Billing data, if you pay us
  • Marketplace listing metadata, if you publish

What we don't

  • App data
  • Files, photos, messages
  • LLM prompts or completions
  • Web traffic content
  • Browsing history
  • Telemetry, analytics, fingerprints

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Flagship (operated by Harry Winner Kamdem, sole proprietorship pending entity formation; the "Service") and to the websites flagshipserver.com and flagship.services, the iOS, Android, and webapp clients, and the marketplace at /marketplace.

It does not cover apps you install on your own Flagship server. Those apps run on hardware you own, under your control, and their privacy practices are theirs. We provide a sandbox; we don't supervise its inhabitants.

It does not cover services you connect through the BYOK / browser features (Anthropic, OpenAI, Amazon, your bank, etc.). When you log into a third-party service from your Flagship browser, that service's privacy policy governs.

2. What we collect, and why

2.1 Account data (necessary)

When you create a Flagship identity, we store a username (your choice, lowercase letters and digits, up to 32 characters), the public key your phone derived for you, and a timestamp. The username is used to route public traffic; the public key verifies that signed actions come from your phone. We never receive or store the private key.

2.2 Sealed unlock blob (necessary)

To let you unlock your server's encrypted disk after a power cycle, your phone uploads a sealed blob. The blob is encrypted to a key only your phone holds; we cannot open it. We hand it back to your server when your phone authorizes the unlock. We retain it as long as you have a paired server.

2.3 Push notification tokens (optional)

If you opt into phone push notifications (server-online, app alerts, security prompts), we store the APNs / FCM token your phone provides. We send these tokens straight to Apple / Google when delivering a notification; we do not retain notification content beyond the time it takes to enqueue.

2.4 Billing data (only if you pay)

If you buy hardware or subscribe to an add-on, Stripe handles payment and we receive: a Stripe customer ID, the last four digits of your card, your billing country, and the line items you bought. We never see the full card number. For shipping hardware we also store your shipping address until the box is delivered, then it's deleted within 90 days.

2.5 Marketplace listing data (only if you publish)

If you publish an app listing, we store the listing fields you submit: title, description, category, screenshots (in R2), the canonical pod URL, your username as author, and timestamps. Listings are public by definition.

2.7 What we explicitly don't collect

  • The content of your TLS-encrypted traffic. flagship.services sees only encrypted bytes; the cert lives on your box.
  • Files, photos, messages, or any application data stored on your Flagship server.
  • LLM prompts, completions, or any data you send through the BYOK provider path.
  • Browsing history from the pod-resident browser feature.
  • Web analytics, ad pixels, session-replay, or browser fingerprints on any of our public pages.

4. Retention

  • Username and public key: kept as long as your account exists. On deletion, removed within 30 days; the public-key fingerprint may persist in revocation lists.
  • Sealed blob: kept while you have a paired server; removed within 30 days of server unpairing.
  • Push tokens: kept while you opt in; removed when you opt out or the token expires.
  • Billing data: kept for 7 years for tax compliance after the last transaction.
  • Shipping addresses: kept until delivered, plus 90 days for return-window handling.
  • Logs: 30 days, then aggregated counters only.
  • Marketplace listings: kept while published; removed on delisting.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data on the following bases:

  • Contract: account data, sealed blobs, server provisioning, billing — necessary to provide what you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest: 30-day operational logs to keep the service running and abuse-resistant. We've balanced this against your interests; you can object.
  • Consent: push notifications, marketing email (if any). Withdrawable from the phone app or by emailing privacy@flagship.services.
  • Legal obligation: tax records on billing data.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights regarding personal data we hold about you:

  • Access: a copy of what we have. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • Correction: fix anything inaccurate.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your account and data; we'll do so within 30 days, except where law (mostly billing records) requires longer retention.
  • Portability: a machine-readable export of your account data.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Restriction: ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, for processing based on consent.

Mail privacy@flagship.services. We'll verify by asking you to sign a challenge with your account key. No friction theatre.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the rights above plus the right to know what categories of personal information we have collected, sold, or shared in the past 12 months. For Flagship, the answers are: we have collected the categories listed in §2; we have not sold or shared personal information; we do not use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We don't track you across the web. We honor GPC by default, mostly because there's nothing to honor — we don't have analytics enabled.

7. International transfers

Cloudflare, Fly, Stripe, and the rest of our sub-processors operate globally. Where data crosses jurisdictions, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (EU) or the equivalent. Your sealed blob is, structurally, opaque to every jurisdiction it passes through.

8. Security

The architecture, threat model, and bug-bounty pointer live at /security. The summary: we hold standard secrets (API keys to our sub-processors) in encrypted vaults, rotate quarterly, scope per-environment. Internal access to operational systems requires hardware-backed FIDO2.

If we ever experience a personal data breach affecting you, we'll notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware, and we'll publish a postmortem with timeline and remediation. We'll also notify any supervisory authority required by law.

9. Children

Flagship is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, mail privacy@flagship.services and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top, post a note in the dashboard, and email account-holders affected. Older versions stay accessible through the GitHub mirror.
