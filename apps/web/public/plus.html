Flagship Plus

Everything that runs on your hardware is free, forever. Plus raises your public-bandwidth allowance — and helps keep Flagship independent. All we need is a username. Not your name.

What you actually pay for

Your apps, your data, your storage, your devices — all on your own box, all free, with no limits we impose. The only thing that costs us money is the shared pipe that carries your public traffic to visitors through our relay. So that's the only thing we meter.

Run as many servers, apps, and devices as you like — those are never counted. You'll only ever see an upgrade prompt if your public traffic genuinely outgrows personal use.

A note on paying privately

If you pay with a card, you become identifiable.

We never ask your name and never see your card. But your card network and bank do keep a record that you paid Flagship — and an adversary who could compel those records could tie your real identity to your account. That's the nature of card rails; it's outside our walls.

If that's in your threat model, pay with cash or Monero instead. No identity is ever attached — we only learn your username, which you chose, and which says nothing about who you are.

This is the same content-blind promise as the rest of Flagship: your box terminates its own TLS, so we can't read your traffic; and your payment, done right, leaves no trail back to you.

How to go Plus

It's hand-operated for now — on purpose. There's no automated checkout collecting your details. You send money, you tell us your username, and we flip your account to Plus by hand.

1 · Tell us your username

The username on your account (the one in <server>.<you>.flagship.services). That's the only thing we need to find your account. Include it with your payment.

2 · Send the money

Cash by mail most private

Fold some cash into a slip of paper with your username and how many months you want, and post it to:

[ your mailing address — fill in before publishing ]

No name needed on the envelope. Like Mullvad's cash-in-an-envelope, this leaves no digital trace at all.

Monero (XMR) private

Send the equivalent of your months to:

[ your Monero address — fill in before publishing ]

Then send your username + the transaction key to harry@flagship.services so we can match it. (Use a throwaway address if you'd rather not link even that.)

Card / Stripe convenient, less private

Coming soon. Convenient, but see the note above — the payment processor will know you're a customer.

A voucher code private

If you were handed a Flagship voucher code, redeem it here (coming soon). A voucher is just prepaid Plus with no identity attached.

3 · We turn on Plus

Once the money lands, we set your account to Plus for the period you paid. Your higher allowance applies within a few minutes of the change. If anything's unclear, write to harry@flagship.services.

Why pay at all, if you're under the limit?

You don't have to — most people never will. But Flagship runs on no ads, no venture capital, and no selling of your data; the relay, DNS, and certificates cost real money to keep up. A Plus membership (even if you never touch the bandwidth) is the cleanest way to keep the project independent and the free tier free for everyone else. Same price, same privacy options — buy it any time, not just when you hit a cap.
