Flagship is a personal-cloud ecosystem. Run servers on commodity hardware, hold the master keys on your phone, and host the apps you build (or vibe-code) on your own infrastructure under your own subdomain.

Three components, one product

The phone app

iOS or Android — Secure Enclave / StrongBox holds the master key. Every sensitive operation is biometric-gated.

The bootable image

Headless NixOS that turns commodity hardware into a node. WiFi credentials and identity are baked in at build time.

The control plane

flagshipserver.com routes traffic by SNI without ever decrypting it. No keys, no secrets, no surveillance.

What makes it different

  • Apps run as containers; persistence goes through one shared Postgres + MinIO + Redis per server, scoped per app — portable migrations, no surprises.
  • You bring your own AI provider (Claude, OpenAI, Google, Ollama). API keys never touch flagshipserver.com.
  • Reciprocal peer-backup with Reed-Solomon erasure coding — your data survives even if your hardware doesn't.
  • Disk encrypted at rest; boot gated by your phone over the internet.
  • Open source. Audit the security model.
Three device families. iOS, Android, and the installable webapp. Each one is its own primary identity — we deliberately don't share keys between them, the way Apple Pay never crosses to Google Pay.
