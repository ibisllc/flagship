Get started

Your stuff, on your hardware, with a real green padlock.

A personal cloud you actually own. Plug it in at home, pair it with your phone, and run the apps you (or your friends) vibe-code in an afternoon.

Three steps. About ten minutes.

01

Pair

Your phone is the keychain. There is no cloud account, no email-and-password, no recovery questions. Just biometrics on a key only you hold.

02

Boot

Plug in any old PC or one of our boxes. One USB stick, one reboot. The phone approves the unlock; your disk encrypts itself.

03

Build

Vibe-code an app from the phone. It runs at yours.flagship.services with a real Let's Encrypt cert. Share with friends in a tap.

We literally cannot read your data.

flagshipserver.com handles identity. flagship.services is a dumb pipe. Your box terminates TLS itself — the green padlock in your browser is signed against a key only your hardware holds.

If we ever get hacked, the worst we can leak about you is your username.

Honest questions.

Why not just use [iCloud / Google / Dropbox]?
Because they read your data. We don't, and structurally we can't. That's the whole product.
What happens when I lose my phone?
If you backed up to iCloud Keychain or Google Block Store, your new phone can recover. If you didn't, your data is gone — like a hardware wallet. We tell you this clearly during setup.
Do I need to be a developer?
No. The phone walks you through it. Vibe-coding an app means typing what you want; the LLM writes it. You'll learn what a Postgres table is by accident, not on purpose.
Can I run this on a Raspberry Pi?
Probably. Anything that can run x86_64 Alpine + docker compose works. We sell pre-built boxes if you want plug-and-go; if you have spare hardware, build your own.
Is this open source?
Yes. BUSL-1.1, becomes Apache 2.0 in 2030. Read the source. Reproducible builds are coming for v1.
How does the marketplace work?
Anyone can list. We host a description, screenshots, and a link to the canonical pod. We don't host code or data. Listings can pay for a security scan (we run npm audit + trivy + semgrep + dynamic checks; you get a letter grade). They can also pay to be featured — clearly labeled.
What if Flagship the company goes away?
Your box keeps working. Your apps keep running. Your TLS cert keeps renewing as long as Let's Encrypt is up and your DNS points home. We don't lock you in because we structurally can't.
