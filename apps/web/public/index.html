What is this?

Flagship is a personal-cloud ecosystem. You run servers on commodity hardware, your phone is the trust root, and the apps you build (or vibe-code) are hosted on your own infrastructure under your own subdomain.

  • Your phone holds the master keys; the desktop UI never gets them.
  • flagshipserver.com is a passthrough — it cannot read your traffic.
  • Disk encrypted at rest; boot gated by your phone over the internet.
  • Open source. Audit the security model.
