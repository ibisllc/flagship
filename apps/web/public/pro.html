Flagship Pro

Flagship is free — genuinely, with no nags and nothing held back. Pro is the membership for the few who are running real things: more public bandwidth, and a way to keep the project independent. All we ever need is a username. Not your name.

Most people never need this

Everything that runs on your hardware — your apps, data, storage, devices — is free, with no limits we impose. The only thing that costs us money is the shared pipe that carries your public traffic to visitors through our relay, so that's the only thing we meter. If your public traffic stays in personal-use range, you'll never see a charge — you may never even notice there's a paid tier at all.

Run as many servers, apps, and devices as you like — those are never counted. Pro is for when your public traffic genuinely outgrows personal use, or when you simply want to back the project.

One-off apps from the Flagship marketplace are a separate thing — you buy those when you want them, not as a subscription. Pro is just about bandwidth headroom and keeping the lights on.

A note on paying privately

If you pay with a card, you become identifiable.

We never ask your name and never see your card. But your card network and bank do keep a record that you paid Flagship — and an adversary who could compel those records could tie your real identity to your account. That's the nature of card rails; it's outside our walls.

If that's in your threat model, pay with cash or Monero instead. No identity is ever attached — we only learn your username, which you chose, and which says nothing about who you are.

It's the same content-blind promise as the rest of Flagship: your box terminates its own TLS, so we can't read your traffic; and your payment, done right, leaves no trail back to you.

How to go Pro

It's hand-operated for now — on purpose. There's no automated checkout collecting your details. You send money, you tell us your username, and we flip your account to Pro by hand.

1 · Tell us your username

The username on your account (the one in <server>.<you>.flagship.services). That's the only thing we need to find your account. Include it with your payment.

2 · Send the money

Cash by mail most private

Fold some cash into a slip of paper with your username and how many months you want, and post it to:

[ your mailing address — fill in before publishing ]

No name needed on the envelope. Like Mullvad's cash-in-an-envelope, this leaves no digital trace at all.

Monero (XMR) private

Send the equivalent of your months to:

[ your Monero address — fill in before publishing ]

Then send your username + the transaction key to harry@flagship.services so we can match it. (Use a throwaway address if you'd rather not link even that.)

Card / Stripe convenient, less private

One-click card checkout. Convenient — but see the note above: the payment processor will know you're a customer.

A voucher code private

If you were handed a Flagship voucher code, redeem it below. A voucher is just prepaid Pro with no identity attached — whoever holds the code redeems it against any username.

3 · We turn on Pro

Once the money lands, we set your account to Pro for the period you paid. Your higher allowance applies within a few minutes of the change. If anything's unclear, write to harry@flagship.services.

Why pay at all, if you're under the limit?

You don't have to — most people never will. But Flagship runs on no ads, no venture capital, and no selling of your data; the relay, DNS, and certificates cost real money to keep up. A Pro membership (even if you never touch the bandwidth) is the cleanest way to keep the project independent and the free tier free for everyone else. Same price, same privacy options — buy it any time, not just when you hit a cap.
