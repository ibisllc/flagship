Flagship Pro

Flagship is free, and the Free plan keeps every feature. Pro and Pro Max add room for public traffic and a few extras. You pay with $1 vouchers that our server signs blind, so it never learns which account a purchase paid for.

What each plan includes

Everything that runs on your own hardware is never counted: servers, apps, data, storage and devices. What we meter is the public traffic our relay carries between your servers and their visitors, because that is what costs us money.

  • Custom domains. Use www.example.com for an app instead of an address under flagship.services. You point a CNAME record at your Flagship address. Your server still holds the certificate, so we still can't read the traffic.
  • Your own outbound proxy. On Pro and Pro Max, your server's traffic can go out through a SOCKS5 or HTTP proxy you choose.

Past 25 GB, a Free account is slowed, not cut off, until the month ends. Most people never get near it.

How to buy

In every app, go to Settings, Plan. You buy a pack of vouchers ($10, $30 or $120), and the app adds it to your account's prepaid balance. Then you choose Pro or Pro Max, and the balance pays for it. Only an admin device of the account can buy, choose a plan or spend the balance.

iPhone and iPad

An in-app purchase through Apple. Apple sees your Apple Account and that you bought Flagship vouchers.

Android

A purchase through Google Play. Google sees your Google account and that you bought Flagship vouchers.

Webapp

A card payment through Stripe, in the webapp. Stripe, your card network and your bank see your card and that you paid Flagship.

What the shops know, and what we don't

Whoever takes your money knows you bought Flagship vouchers. Vouchers keep that out of our records, not out of theirs. None of them ever learns your Flagship account, and we never learn which purchase paid for which account.

Vouchers: anonymous by design

A voucher is worth $1. When you buy, your device makes the vouchers and our server signs them blind: it checks the payment and signs, without seeing the vouchers it signed. When a voucher is spent later, nothing ties it to that payment. Flagship can't link a payment to an account.

  • Your admin devices hold the wallet. The balance is kept encrypted under your account's key. Our server stores it for your devices to sync, and can't open it. Vouchers don't expire.
  • They spend a little at a time. There is no monthly charge. Your device spends one $1 voucher at a random moment, keeping your plan a few days ahead. One voucher buys 3 days of Pro or 36 hours of Pro Max, so every account's spends look the same.
  • A 72-hour grace period. If your devices are off when a spend is due, your plan keeps working for 72 hours past its end. A spend in that time continues from where the plan ended: nothing lapses, and nothing is free.
  • A random start, if you want one. Our server can see a purchase and an upgrade that happen close together. If that matters to you, start the plan at a random time within the next 24 hours.
  • A private relay for spends. Spends go through a relay, so our server doesn't see your network address when you pay. For now Flagship runs that relay itself; it isn't an independent operator yet.

What we do see: each account's plan and the date it is paid until, because the relay needs them to count traffic. Never what paid for it.

More data

Past the traffic a plan includes, Pro and Pro Max can buy more in blocks of 100 GB for $5, paid from the balance. A block is bought automatically when less than 10 GB is left for the month, up to a monthly cap you set: $10 by default. Past the cap your traffic is slowed, as on Free. We never bill you after the fact.

Refunds

We don't give refunds. The honest reason: vouchers are built so that nothing links them back to the payment that bought them. Once they are signed, we can't tell which vouchers a payment produced, so we can't take them back or pay them out.

Purchases made through Apple or Google Play follow those stores' own refund rules, which we don't control.

Why pay at all?

You don't have to, and most people never will. Flagship runs with no ads, no venture capital and no selling of your data; the relay, DNS and certificates cost real money to keep up. A plan, even one you never fill, keeps the project independent and the free tier free for everyone else.
