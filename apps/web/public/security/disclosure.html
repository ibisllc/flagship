Coordinated disclosure & bounty

Flagship is a security-first product. We pay for serious vulnerabilities in a defined scope and respond to reports under a published SLA. Coordinated disclosure is required — exploit details stay private until a fix ships.

Scope

In scope

  • flagshipserver.com — the Cloudflare Worker, all /api/* routes, the static site.
  • web.flagshipserver.com — the webapp PWA: WebAuthn / IRK signing / lease deposit / recovery upload paths.
  • *.flagship.services — the SNI passthrough plane on Fly, the tunnel hub.
  • The Flagship daemon as shipped in the canonical Alpine ISO (flagship-base-alpine-3.21.0-x86_64.iso). Includes ACME, the local TLS server, the data layer (Postgres / MinIO / Redis / Forgejo / Chromium under /var/flagship/data/).
  • The canonical-bytes layer in @flagship/protocol — sign/verify routines, key derivations, encryption.
  • iOS and Android apps once shipped to TestFlight / Play Console.

Out of scope

  • Vulnerabilities in apps installed by individual users (those are the app author's responsibility; report to them directly via the app's marketplace listing).
  • Self-hosted forks of Flagship (only the canonical builds are in scope).
  • Issues requiring physical access to a user's already-unlocked phone or laptop.
  • Theoretical attacks against well-vetted primitives (Ed25519, X25519, AES-128-GCM, SHA-256, HKDF) without a working PoC.
  • Rate-limiting, denial-of-service, or volumetric attacks against public endpoints. (We rely on Cloudflare's edge for these.)
  • Missing security headers on static pages where there's no XSS sink.
  • Reports auto-generated by scanners with no manual analysis.

Bounty payouts

SeverityExamplesRange (USD)
Critical Account takeover without phone access; arbitrary unlock-key extraction; remote code execution on the daemon; canonical-bytes signature bypass. $5,000 – $20,000
High Cross-user data exposure; bypass of paired-session auth; unauthenticated read of auto_unlock_leases or webauthn_recovery_records contents (cleartext). $1,000 – $5,000
Medium Stored or reflected XSS on flagshipserver.com or web.flagshipserver.com; CSRF on a state-changing endpoint not protected by signed canonical-bytes; significant trust-model violation that doesn't directly leak keys. $250 – $1,000
Low Open redirect; subdomain takeover on a non-served subdomain; privacy leak (e.g. timing-side-channel revealing whether a username exists) without further impact. $50 – $250

Final amount depends on impact, exploitability, the quality of the report, and whether you provided a working PoC. We reserve the right to award above the listed range for exceptional reports. Payouts are in USD via bank transfer or sent to a Bitcoin / Ethereum address you provide.

SLA

  • Initial acknowledgement: ≤ 2 business days
  • Triage decision (in/out of scope, severity): ≤ 7 days
  • Fix ship target by severity:
    • Critical: ≤ 7 days
    • High: ≤ 30 days
    • Medium: ≤ 90 days
    • Low: best-effort, usually rolled into the next release.
  • Public disclosure (reporter credit): coordinated with you, after the fix has shipped to all auto-update channels.

How to report

File via the vulnerability report form (preferred — it nudges the right structured fields), or email security@flagshipserver.com. Encrypt sensitive details with our PGP key (download from /security.html; fingerprint posted there and on /.well-known/security.txt).

Please include:

  • A clear description of the issue.
  • The exact endpoint / file / commit hash affected.
  • A working PoC (script, curl invocation, or screenshots).
  • Your assessed impact and severity.
  • Whether you'd like to be credited publicly (and under what name).

Safe-harbor

Good-faith research conducted under this policy will not be subject to legal action by Flagship. You may probe production endpoints provided you don't:

  • Access, modify, or delete other users' data beyond the minimum needed to demonstrate the issue.
  • Use the issue to extract more than test-account data.
  • Run automated scanners that generate sustained load.
  • Publish details before the fix has shipped, or before our mutually agreed disclosure date.
