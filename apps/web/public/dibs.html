Own the .com? Claim your name first.

Every Flagship account gets a random name to start with, and anyone can switch to a name of their own. For the first year, a name that matches a registered .com is held for whoever controls that domain.

How it works

  • .com only. If you control acme.com, you can claim the Flagship name acme. Other endings (.net, .org, country domains) get no special claim.
  • Prove it, once. In the app, go to Settings → Claim your .com name. You'll get a short code to publish in one of two places:
    • a DNS TXT record at _flagship-claim.acme.com, or
    • a text file at https://acme.com/.well-known/flagship-claim.
    The code is tied to your account's key, so nobody else can reuse it.
  • First proof wins. If two accounts prove the same domain, the first verified claim gets the name. There is no appeal process.
  • $20, once. Claiming a held name costs $20 (an ordinary name change is $10). Your servers move to the new name with you.

Everyone else

You can buy any name that's free and isn't held for a .com holder, any time — including during the window. When the year ends, held names open up to everyone, first come, first served.

Why

Names on Flagship are yours for good — we never take one back. Giving domain holders a head start means nobody can grab a brand's name before the brand has heard of us, without us ever having to judge who deserves a name.
